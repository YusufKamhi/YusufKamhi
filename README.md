## 👨‍💻 About Me

29+ years building software at the intersection of **logistics**, **customs compliance**, and **AI-augmented engineering** — from writing Turkey's first NCTS transit declaration systems at Ulukom in the late 90s, to leading multi-agent AI workflows at WiseTech Global today.

I lead the **Turkey Customs Team** at WiseTech, delivering CargoWise modules for Declaration, Summary Declaration, Export Union, eCommerce, and NCTS. I care deeply about architectural integrity, TDD, CI/CD, and building engineering cultures where people grow.

Outside code: **European Commission External Expert** (Horizon 2020, 10+ years), **TÜBİTAK Business Development Mentor**, and occasional philosopher.
